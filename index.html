Evolution-inspired neurobiology

We study how nervous systems are built, function, and evolve.

Research

We study early-diverging nervous systems such as Hydra, combining molecular, physiological, and engineering approaches to uncover fundamental mechanisms of neural development, neuronal computation, and nervous system evolution.

From Nerve Nets to Organized Nervous Systems

How can a diffuse nerve net generate coordinated and complex behavior?

We connect neuronal architecture with circuit function and behavior, and investigate how distributed neuronal populations become organized into specialized structures during evolution.

Gut–Nerve Communication

What were the original functions of nervous systems in gut regulation?

We study how neurons sense and regulate internal physiological states, and how ancient gut, epithelial, and neuronal communication systems evolved.
