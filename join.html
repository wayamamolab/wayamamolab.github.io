Join Us

The lab is recruiting graduate students!

We welcome people from all backgrounds who enjoy conducting research. We investigate how nervous systems acquire their structure through development, evolution, and engineering. We value exploratory science, pursuing intriguing and serendipitous observations to uncover the fundamental mechanisms underlying them.

Recruiting

Graduate Students

Experience in molecular biology, neuroimaging, bioinformatics, or related fields is preferred but not required.

Prospective PhD students can apply through UA Little Rock’s Applied Science PhD program (Applied Biosciences), including directly after a bachelor’s degree. Please contact Wataru before applying.

Recruiting

Undergraduate students

Motivated UA Little Rock undergraduates are welcome as space allows. No previous research experience is needed—just curiosity, reliability, and a willingness to learn.

Please tell us a little about your interests and availability.

Future opportunities

Postdocs & research staff

Postdoctoral, technician, staff scientist, and post-baccalaureate openings will depend on future funding.

Prospective postdocs interested in pursuing fellowships are welcome to get in touch.

How to apply

Email wyamamoto1@ualr.edu with a one-page description of your research experience and interests. If you are new to research, tell us what questions you would like to explore.

Admission and funding are subject to university review and availability.
