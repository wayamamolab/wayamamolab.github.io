Wataru Yamamoto, PhD

Principal Investigator

Wataru is an Assistant Professor in the Department of Biology at the University of Arkansas at Little Rock. He is interested in how nervous systems acquire their organization, function, and evolve.

Wataru completed his undergraduate studies at Indiana University and pursued his PhD with Dr. Carmella Evans-Molina, studying the role of endoplasmic reticulum (ER) calcium channels in diabetes. He then joined Dr. Rafael Yuste’s lab at Columbia University, where he became fascinated by the nervous system and behaviors of Hydra. Before joining UA Little Rock, he was a Grass Fellow, exploring interoception in Hydra.

Throughout his career, Wataru has worked with a variety of experimental systems, including crayfish, Daphnia, Drosophila, mice, human cells, and Hydra. These experiences have shaped his appreciation for the unique opportunities that different model organisms offer for fundamental biological discovery.
